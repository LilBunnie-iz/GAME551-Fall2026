A note about me

Character Design Reel Breakdown

Kiki Ironton: This concept was created as a challenge for myself to redesign a character from 2017. I went with a low poly 2000s style design. This character was featured in an exhibition called Concept Art in 2025.

Chibby Design 1: I was in charge of designing the main character for an Animation Capstone Project along with two other students. My job was to come up with a guinea pig design, this concept is meant to be zany and more cartoonish.

Chibby Design 2: The second design was meant to look like a more cute and realistic sort of idea.

Chibby Design 3: The last one was my idea of combining the first two designs together.

Chibby Turnaround Design Sketch: We ended up choosing Chibby Design 1 as the final design. I had the responsibility of making the turnarounds for the character.

Chibby Turnaround Design: I was in charge of taking the sketch and applying it to digital.

Chibby Facial Expressions: I was also instructed with making expression sheets for all the characters.

Scruffy Turnaround Sketch: Another character I added for the Capstone Project is Scruffy who chases after Chibby as he tries to escape. Unlike Chibby’s concepts, Scruffy only had one design, which stood out immediately to the group.

Scruffy Turnaround Final Design: I was tasked to come up with a color scheme for this furry fellow. I went with colors that were inspired by Lucifer from Cinderella and Butch from Tom and Jerry.

Scruffy Facial Expressions: I was tasked to do an expression sheet for Scruffy as well. I wanted him to look more cartoony with Tom from Tom and Jerry being the big inspiration.

Lucas: Lucas is a character I made for a storyboard assignment where we had to make one about first-person point of view. I decided to go for a video game type of story, where you play as this creature who defeats enemies and saves the world.

The Baconator: For the same assignment, The Baconator is the main villain of the concept. He is the exact opposite of Lucas, he wants to control the world.

Oakley's Garden: Oakley's Garden is a concept environment design for the Kiki and Starla franchise. I used both Adobe Photoshop and Animate to create this design.

Mission Inc Plush Concept: Mission Inc was a story concept I made where the side villains were main characters, they are tasked to take something in their assigned locations. At the end of every story, they're captured and go to jail. These plush concepts were designed for a practice advertising assignment which was about presenting yourself and creating concepts of merchandise.
